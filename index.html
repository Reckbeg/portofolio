Context and my role

Payroll support, leave, and attendance often begin in spreadsheets. I use hands-on HR experience to define each template's scope, structure the information, and maintain the free library for small teams.

Nine downloadable XLSX templates covering payroll, leave, attendance, tax, THR, BPJS, performance, employee data, overtime, and turnover.

  • Direct access with no login or registration.
  • Employee data stays in the downloaded spreadsheet.
